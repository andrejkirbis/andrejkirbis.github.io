Projects

Research Projects

Involvement in national and international research projects spanning the core research areas.

Political Polarization and Democratic Participation

Research examining the relationship between political polarization and civic engagement, with a focus on affective and ideological divides and their consequences for democratic participation in European societies.

Political Sociology Polarization Democracy

Vaccine Hesitancy and Health Literacy

Investigation of the social, political, and cultural determinants of vaccine hesitancy and health literacy, drawing on survey data from Slovenia and comparative European sources.

Health Sociology Vaccination Public Health

Youth Values and Political Participation

Study of the political attitudes, values, and participation patterns of young people in Slovenia and cross-nationally, with attention to generational change and political socialisation.

Youth Studies Political Participation Values
