Andrej
Kirbiš

Full Professor of Sociology

University of Maribor · Faculty of Arts · Slovenia

My research examines political participation and polarization, health sociology, vaccine hesitancy and health literacy, youth studies, environmental attitudes and behaviour, cultural participation, volunteering, and civic engagement in Slovenia and across Europe. I use quantitative methods including multilevel modelling, structural equation modelling, cross-lagged panel analysis, and comparative and longitudinal survey designs.

Research Areas

My empirical research programme spans political behaviour, health attitudes, youth, environmental engagement, and civic life, grounded in comparative and longitudinal quantitative methods.

01
Political Participation & Polarization

Affective and ideological polarization, political trust, democratic engagement, cross-national comparisons.

02
Health Sociology & Vaccine Hesitancy

Vaccination attitudes, health literacy, trust in science and healthcare, health inequalities, longitudinal dynamics.

03
Health, Inequalities & Lifestyles

Dietary patterns and behaviour, substance use, mental health, socioeconomic position, education, and health outcomes.

04
Youth Studies

Political orientation and participation among youth, health, values, educational inequalities, and cross-national differences.

05
Environmental Attitudes & Climate Literacy

Climate knowledge, pro-environmental behaviour, sustainable diets, environmental participation, and cross-national research.

06
Volunteering, Cultural Participation & Civil Society

Cultural engagement, volunteering determinants and barriers, social capital, participation inequalities, and civic engagement in Slovenia and Europe.

07
Comparative & Longitudinal Quantitative Research

Comparative and longitudinal survey research; cross-national analysis, panel data, multilevel modelling, structural equation modelling, cross-lagged panel analysis, mediation and moderation, latent profile analysis.
