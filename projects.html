Funded Research

Projects

I have led and participated in national, bilateral, and international research projects covering political participation and affective polarization, health sociology, vaccine hesitancy and health literacy, civic engagement and volunteering, youth studies, environmental behaviour and climate literacy, cultural participation, and comparative survey research in Slovenia and Central and Eastern Europe.

Active

Current Projects

Nationwide Slovenian Study on the Impact of Volunteering on Society: Determinants, Barriers, Consequences, and Policy Recommendations

I lead this national project examining the prevalence, determinants, barriers, and societal consequences of volunteering in Slovenia, including its role in social capital and civic engagement. The project combines a multi-wave national panel study, comparative international analyses, and qualitative research and aims to develop evidence-based recommendations for policy and practice.

Health and Political Participation: A Panel Study of Slovenian Youth and Adults

I lead this bilateral project examining relationships between health and political participation using longitudinal Slovenian panel data, with particular attention to age, health inequalities, and political engagement.

Beyond One-Size-Fits-All: A Person-Oriented, Longitudinal, and Cross-National Approach to Vaccine Hesitancy

I participate in this project investigating heterogeneity and change in vaccine hesitancy through person-oriented, longitudinal, and cross-national approaches, with attention to health literacy and trust in science as key determinants. Principal Investigator: Monika Lamot.

I led the Slovenian part of this comparative project examining political participation, democratic engagement, and civic engagement in Central and Eastern Europe — Austria, Poland, and Slovenia.
