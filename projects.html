Projects

Research initiatives spanning political sociology, health sociology, youth studies, volunteering, cultural participation, and comparative public opinion research.

Research Projects

Political Polarization and Democratic Participation

Research examining the relationship between political polarization and civic engagement, with a focus on affective and ideological divides and their consequences for democratic participation and political trust in European societies.

Political Sociology Polarization Democracy

Vaccine Hesitancy and Health Literacy

Investigation of the social, political, and cultural determinants of vaccine hesitancy and health literacy. Research draws on survey data to examine the role of values, trust in institutions, and social identities in shaping vaccination attitudes.

Health Sociology Vaccine Hesitancy Public Health

Youth Values and Political Participation

Study of the political attitudes, civic values, and participation patterns of young people in Slovenia and cross-nationally. Research explores generational change, political socialisation, and the factors shaping youth civic engagement.

Youth Studies Political Participation Values

Cultural Participation of Young People

Research on the cultural engagement of young people — including attendance at cultural events, participation in arts, and access to cultural resources — and its relationship with social inequalities and civic identity.

Cultural Participation Youth Studies Social Inequality

Volunteering in Slovenia

Analysis of the scope, drivers, and trends of formal and informal volunteering in Slovenian society. Research includes comparative European perspectives and examines sociodemographic predictors and the social significance of voluntary activity.

Volunteering Civil Society Slovenia

Comparative Cross-National Public Opinion Research

Longitudinal and comparative research using major international survey programmes — including the European Social Survey, European Values Study, and ISSP — to examine public opinion dynamics and value change across European societies.

Public Opinion Comparative Research ESS · EVS · ISSP
