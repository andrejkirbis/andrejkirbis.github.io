Projects

I have led and participated in national, bilateral, and international research projects in political sociology, health sociology, youth studies, environmental behaviour, volunteering, cultural participation, and comparative social research. My recent projects use longitudinal panel surveys, cross-national comparative data, and quantitative and qualitative research designs.
