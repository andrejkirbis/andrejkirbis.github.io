Publications

My publications address political participation and polarization, health sociology and vaccine hesitancy, health literacy and health inequalities, youth studies, environmental attitudes and behaviour, cultural participation, and comparative quantitative social research. My work includes peer-reviewed journal articles, scientific monographs, book chapters, systematic reviews, and edited volumes.

Author name note: My publications may appear under Kirbiš, A. or Kirbis, A. (without diacritic) in bibliographic databases.
