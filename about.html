Biography

Academic Profile

Andrej Kirbiš is Full Professor of Sociology at the Department of Sociology, Faculty of Arts, University of Maribor, Slovenia.

His research focuses on political sociology, public opinion, political participation, political polarization, social values, health sociology, vaccination attitudes, youth studies, environmental attitudes, volunteering, cultural participation, and comparative cross-national research. He has authored numerous peer-reviewed scientific publications and leads or participates in several national and international research projects.

Teaching

Teaching activities at the University of Maribor cover sociology, political sociology, social research methodology, quantitative research methods, and related courses at undergraduate and postgraduate level.
