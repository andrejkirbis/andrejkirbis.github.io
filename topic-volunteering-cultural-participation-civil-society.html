Civil Society Topic

Volunteering, Cultural Participation and Civil Society

Research on volunteering, cultural participation, social capital, civic engagement, participation inequalities, and formal and informal participation in Slovenia and Europe.

Overview

Civic and Cultural Participation Research

This topic covers volunteering, cultural engagement, civil society, social capital, participation inequalities, and barriers and motivations for civic participation.

Work on cultural participation has examined patterns, trends, determinants, and consequences of cultural participation among young people in Slovenia and Europe.

Current project work extends this research to the prevalence, determinants, barriers, and consequences of formal and informal volunteering in Slovenia.

Nationwide Slovenian Study on the Impact of Volunteering on Society: Determinants, Barriers, Consequences, and Policy Recommendations. Code V5-2519. ARIS & Ministry of Public Administration. 2025–2027.

Focus: This current national project examines the prevalence, determinants, barriers, and societal consequences of volunteering in Slovenia, including its role in social capital and civic engagement. It combines a multi-wave national panel study, comparative international analyses, and qualitative research.
