Research

My research examines how social position, political context, values, knowledge, trust, and institutional environments shape political, health, civic, and environmental attitudes and behaviour. I primarily use quantitative survey research, including cross-national comparisons and longitudinal panel designs.

Research Areas

1

Political Participation & Polarization

Political participation, ideological and affective polarization, political trust, and democratic engagement

My research examines how different forms of political participation are associated with polarization, political information, trust, health, and individual and contextual resources. My recent work investigates the relationship between affective polarization and political participation, the role of ideological polarization in the relationship between political news consumption and participation across 24 countries, and longitudinal relationships between perceived ideological polarization, institutional trust, and vaccination attitudes.

Political Participation Affective Polarization Ideological Polarization Political Trust Political News Democratic Engagement
2

Health Sociology, Vaccine Hesitancy & Health Literacy

Social inequalities, trust, health literacy, vaccination attitudes, and health behaviour

A major strand of my research investigates vaccine hesitancy, vaccination intentions, health literacy, trust in healthcare and science, health inequalities, and health-related behaviour. I examine individual and contextual determinants including socioeconomic position, education, cultural capital, conspiracy mentality, ideological polarization, institutional trust, and national economic and cultural characteristics.

My recent work also examines the measurement of health literacy and vaccination readiness, differences between groups with different patterns of vaccine hesitancy, and longitudinal changes in vaccination attitudes.

Vaccine Hesitancy Health Literacy Health Inequalities Institutional Trust Vaccination Attitudes Health Behaviour
3

Health, Social Inequalities & Lifestyles

Health status, diet, substance use, mental health, and socioeconomic inequalities

I examine how education, socioeconomic position, gender, health literacy, and other social resources are associated with health and health-related behaviour. My work in this area includes research on fruit and vegetable intake, meat consumption and reduction, sustainable dietary behaviour, adolescent substance use, mental health, and relationships between physical health and political participation.

Health Inequalities Dietary Behaviour Substance Use Mental Health Education Socioeconomic Position
4

Youth Studies

Political attitudes, participation, health, values, and inequalities among young people

Young people are a recurring population in my research. I study political orientation and participation among youth, health and substance use, environmental attitudes, cultural participation, educational inequalities, values, and cross-national differences among young people in Slovenia and other European societies.

My youth research draws on both national and comparative datasets and examines how socioeconomic resources, family background, education, gender, and wider social contexts are related to attitudes and behaviour.

Youth Participation Political Orientation Health Social Inequalities Cultural Participation Environmental Attitudes
5

Environmental Attitudes, Climate Literacy & Behaviour

Climate knowledge, environmental engagement, dietary behaviour, and inequalities

My environmental research examines climate literacy, environmental attitudes, environmentally friendly behaviour, and environmental civic engagement. My recent work investigates the measurement of climate knowledge, the relationship between education and active environmental engagement, climate literacy as a mechanism linking education with meat consumption and reduction, and cross-national environmental public sphere behaviour.

Climate Literacy Environmental Attitudes Pro-environmental Behaviour Sustainable Diets Environmental Participation
6

Cultural Participation, Volunteering & Civil Society

Cultural engagement, volunteering, participation inequalities, and civic life

I study cultural participation and volunteering as important dimensions of civic and social engagement. My work examines participation patterns, motivations and barriers, inequalities in access and engagement, and traditional and digital forms of cultural participation.

My research project on cultural participation among young people combined national and comparative European analyses. My current national project on volunteering extends this research to the prevalence, determinants, barriers, and consequences of formal and informal volunteering in Slovenia.

Volunteering Cultural Participation Civil Society Participation Inequalities Civic Engagement
7

Comparative & Longitudinal Quantitative Research

Cross-national survey analysis, panel data, multilevel modelling, and structural equation modelling

Much of my research uses comparative and quantitative designs. I analyse national and international survey data to examine individual and contextual differences and increasingly use longitudinal panel data to investigate change over time and relationships between variables across multiple waves.

Methods used in my recent research include multilevel modelling, structural equation modelling, cross-lagged panel analysis, moderated mediation, latent profile analysis, psychometric validation, discrete choice experiments, and systematic review methodology.
