Research

Research programme spanning political sociology, health sociology, youth studies, environmental attitudes, and comparative quantitative social science.

Research Areas

1

Political Sociology & Polarization

Political identities, ideological divides, and affective polarization

Research in this area examines political identities, ideological divides, partisan sorting, and affective polarization in European societies. The focus is on understanding how political attitudes are structured, how they change over time, and what social factors drive increasing polarisation in contemporary democracies.

Affective Polarization Ideological Divides Party Identification Democratic Attitudes
2

Political Participation

Conventional, unconventional, and digital civic engagement

This strand investigates conventional and unconventional forms of political engagement — including voting, party membership, protest, and digital activism — as well as attitudes toward democratic institutions, political trust, and civic norms across European societies.

Voting Civic Engagement Political Trust Digital Participation Civil Society
3

Public Opinion & Social Values

Value orientations, attitudinal change, and cross-national patterns

Using large-scale cross-national surveys (ESS, EVS, ISSP), this research examines the structure and dynamics of public opinion, value orientations, postmaterialist shifts, and attitudinal change across European societies over time.

Value Change Social Attitudes ESS EVS ISSP
4

Health Sociology & Vaccine Hesitancy

Social determinants of health behaviour and vaccination attitudes

Research in health sociology addresses the social determinants of health behaviour, with particular attention to vaccine hesitancy, trust in medical institutions, and the role of social values, media consumption, and political identity in shaping attitudes toward vaccination.

Vaccine Hesitancy Health Behaviour Medical Trust COVID-19 Public Health
5

Youth Studies

Political socialisation, youth values, and generational change

This area investigates young people's political socialisation, civic values, and participation in public life. Research draws on national and comparative data to explore how younger generations differ from older cohorts in their political attitudes, social values, and civic engagement.

Political Socialisation Youth Participation Generational Analysis Life Course
6

Environmental Sociology

Ecological values, climate concern, and pro-environmental behaviour

Research in environmental sociology examines ecological values, climate change concern, and the social, political, and cultural factors that shape pro-environmental attitudes and behaviour in comparative perspective.

Environmental Attitudes Climate Change Ecological Values Green Behaviour
7

Volunteering & Cultural Participation

Civil society engagement, associational life, and cultural activity

This area focuses on formal and informal volunteering, associational membership, and cultural participation as dimensions of civil society engagement. Research explores sociodemographic predictors of volunteering and cultural activity in Slovenia and cross-nationally.

Volunteering Cultural Engagement Civil Society Civic Participation
8

Comparative Research & Quantitative Methods

Cross-national survey analysis and advanced statistical modelling

Substantive research is grounded in rigorous quantitative methodology, including structural equation modelling, multilevel analysis, and secondary analysis of major survey programmes. Research draws on ESS, EVS, ISSP, and other large-scale international datasets to examine cross-national and longitudinal patterns.

Structural Equation Modelling Multilevel Modelling Cross-National Comparison Longitudinal Analysis Survey Methodology
